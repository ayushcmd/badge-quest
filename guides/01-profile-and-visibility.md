# Profile and Visibility

## Where badges appear

Open your profile at `github.com/<your-username>`. Earned achievements appear in the left sidebar below your bio. Click a badge to see details and sometimes the events that triggered it.

## Show or hide all achievements

1. Click your profile picture in the top right corner, then click Settings.
2. Under Profile settings, select or clear Show Achievements on my profile.
3. Click Update preferences.

## Hide one badge

1. Open your profile.
2. Under Achievements, click the badge you want to hide.
3. Click the eye icon.

A hidden badge is visible only to you.

## Private contributions

By default only your public work counts. To include private and internal repositories:

1. Open your profile.
2. Above the contribution calendar, click Contribution settings.
3. Turn on Private contributions.

Private activity is shown in anonymized form, without repository names.

Different guides describe these switches with slightly different names, and the labels in GitHub's interface can change. The two ideas to remember are one switch for whether achievements are shown at all, and one switch for whether private contributions are included. GitHub's own documentation is the reference: [Manage visibility settings for private contributions and achievements](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/how-tos/contribution-settings/manage-visibility-settings-for-private-contributions-and-achievements).

## Check your Git email

Commits are matched to your account by email.

```bash
git config user.email
```

That address must be listed and verified under Settings, Emails. If it is not, add it and verify it. Otherwise your commits may not be linked to you, and badges that depend on them will not appear.

## Emoji skin tone

Pick your preferred skin tone in the Appearance settings. Some badges use it.
