# Galaxy Brain

## The rule

Have your replies accepted as answers in GitHub Discussions.

## Tiers

| Default | Bronze | Silver | Gold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 8 | 16 | 32 |

The number is how many of your replies were marked as the answer.

## Difficulty

Medium to hard. Someone else has to accept your answer.

## Important change

GitHub stopped awarding this badge for answers in its own Community forum, according to replies from GitHub staff in that forum. Accepted answers in the Discussions of other repositories still count. Criteria can change again, so check the sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Steps

1. Find projects that have Discussions turned on with a Question and Answer category. Look at the Discussions tab of tools you already use.
2. Pick questions you can really answer. Read the whole question, and reproduce the problem if it involves code.
3. Write a helpful reply: explain why it happens, show the fix, and link the relevant documentation.
4. Wait for the person who asked, or a maintainer, to mark your reply as the answer.
5. Repeat until you have two accepted answers, then wait up to 24 hours.

More about Discussions: [About Discussions](https://docs.github.com/en/discussions/collaborating-with-your-community-using-discussions/about-discussions).

## Common mistakes

- Posting quick, low-effort replies that do not solve the problem. They do not get accepted.
- Trying to answer your own question. The point is helping someone else.
- Looking only in GitHub's own Community forum, which no longer counts.

## Fair play

Answer only what you actually know. Posting guesses across many threads to farm accepted answers is spam, and it is the reason this badge was restricted in the first place. See [11-fair-play.md](11-fair-play.md).
