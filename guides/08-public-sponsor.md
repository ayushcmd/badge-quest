# Public Sponsor

## The rule

Sponsor a contributor or project through GitHub Sponsors, and keep the sponsorship public.

## Tiers

None. Public Sponsor is a one-time badge. It was previously named GitHub Sponsor.

## Difficulty

Very easy, but it costs real money.

## Steps

1. Decide whether you want to sponsor anyone. This is optional, and nobody needs to pay for a badge.
2. Browse people and projects at [github.com/sponsors/explore](https://github.com/sponsors/explore). Start with tools you rely on every day.
3. Choose a sponsorship tier and complete the payment. Some maintainers offer one-time options and others offer monthly ones.
4. When asked, choose to make the sponsorship public. A private sponsorship does not show on your profile.
5. Wait up to 24 hours.

## Common mistakes

- Sponsoring privately and then wondering why nothing shows.
- Forgetting a monthly sponsorship is recurring.
- Treating it as a shortcut. Sponsor because you want to support the maintainer.

## Good news

If money is tight, skip this badge. Every other badge is earned with time and effort, not payment.
